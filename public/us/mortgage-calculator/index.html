US Mortgage Calculator (PITI)

Understanding your PITI payment (Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance) is the first step toward responsible homeownership. Our tool provides a professional-grade estimate updated for 2026 standards.

The Amortization Formula

We use the standard banking formula to ensure 100% accuracy:

M = P × [ r(1 + r)n ] / [ (1 + r)n − 1 ]

Principal & Interest

The "P&I" is the base of your loan. In the early years, your payments mostly cover interest. Over 30 years, this flips as you build equity.

Taxes & Insurance

Most calculators ignore these, but they can add 20-30% to your actual monthly cost. We include local property tax estimates and homeowners insurance to give you the truth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PITI?

It stands for Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance—the four pillars of a mortgage payment.

When does PMI stop?

Typically when your loan-to-value ratio reaches 80% (20% equity).
